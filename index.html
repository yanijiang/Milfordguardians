Protecting Milford Sound

Discover the beauty, history, and challenges of Milford Sound. Join us in preserving this natural wonder for future generations.

Natural Beauty

Milford Sound, located in Fiordland National Park, is one of New Zealand’s most stunning natural wonders. Surrounded by towering cliffs, lush rainforests, and cascading waterfalls like Stirling and Bowen Falls, it’s home to incredible wildlife, including fur seals, bottlenose dolphins, Fiordland crested penguins, and native birds. The deep waters and steep mountains create a unique and peaceful environment, attracting visitors from all over the world who come to admire its raw beauty by boat, kayak, or on foot.

Challenges

Milford Sound is a place of incredible natural beauty and deep historical and cultural value. But as tourism grows and environmental pressures increase, this iconic location faces real challenges — including pollution, noise, and harm to native species. This website shares the story of Milford Sound’s past, explains the issues it faces today, and offers practical ways we can all help reduce our impact and support its long-term protection.
